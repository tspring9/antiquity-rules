Rome

Senatus Populusque Romanus

Capital

Roma

On the Tiber in Latium

  • 2 gold each turn
  • Usually a stronghold
  • Often a victory objective

Heavy infantry

Legion

Manipular. +1 Attack while your infantry line is not full. At a stronghold the attacker's 4-slot line is never full, so Legions storm at +1 the whole assault.

How Rome plays

Legions get stronger as the line thins, which makes Rome the best power at storming cities. Bring a Siege Engine, breach the walls, and let the Manipular bonus carry the assault.

Carthage

  • Often a victory objective

How Carthage plays

Carthage's infantry has no trick of its own, so its edge comes from gold, ships and War Elephants. An elephant in the Cavalry lane beside the General attacks at 6, and it takes two hits to bring down.

Ptolemaic Egypt

The house of Ptolemy

Capital

Alexandria

Harbour city of the Pharos on the Nile delta

  • 2 gold each turn
  • Usually a stronghold
  • Often a victory objective

Heavy infantry

Hoplite

Phalanx. +1 Attack and Defense while all 5 infantry slots are filled (Levies count). Checked at the start of each round. Never applies when assaulting a stronghold.

How Ptolemaic Egypt plays

Keep all five infantry slots filled and every Hoplite rolls 5 on attack and defense. Cheap Levies hold the line, and heavy reserves refill the gaps. A full phalanx behind Alexandria's walls is the hardest garrison in the game.
