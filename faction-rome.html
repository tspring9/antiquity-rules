Legion

Manipular. +1 Attack while your infantry line is not full. At a stronghold the attacker's 4-slot line is never full, so Legions storm at +1 the whole assault.

How Rome plays

Legions get stronger as the line thins, which makes Rome the best power at storming cities. Bring a Siege Engine, breach the walls, and let the Manipular bonus carry the assault.
