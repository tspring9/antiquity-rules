Each round

  • Check: line full? General posted?
  • Roll a d12 per front slot. Target or lower hits.
  • Hits go straight ahead, then roll down the lane.
  • Remove losses, refill from reserve.
  • Attacker may retreat.

Where hits spill

  • Empty lane at round start: Infantry → Cavalry → Flank Inf. → Flank Cav.
  • Reserves are never hit.
  • Generals are struck last, when nothing else stands.
  • Attacker's Flank Infantry is reached only by spillover.
